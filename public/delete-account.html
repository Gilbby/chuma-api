Delete your account

Last updated: 18 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Chuma account and personal data at any time. This page explains how to do it inside the app, and how to request deletion if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Chuma app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Legal and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is removed immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't use the app, email us from the phone number or email associated with your account and we will delete it for you. Please include enough detail to verify your identity (your registered mobile number and full name).

Email a deletion request

Or write to contact@bristalite.com. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and login (name, phone number, profile photo, app PIN).
  • Your identity verification (KYC) details, including ID number and date of birth.
  • Your payment details (payout method and account number).
  • Your notifications and one-time sign-in codes.

What may be kept

To keep each savings group's own books complete and accurate after you leave, a group's historical records may retain only your name against past contributions, loans, and repayments. These records no longer link back to you (your phone number and account are removed) and are retained for the group's own audit and legal purposes.

Please settle up first. Because Chuma holds money on members' behalf, we can't delete your account while it still has money or responsibilities attached — for example an active loan you still owe, savings you still hold in a group, or being the sole chairperson of a group other members depend on. The app will tell you exactly what to resolve first (repay the loan, leave the group to be refunded, or hand over the chairperson role). Once those are settled, deletion goes through.
