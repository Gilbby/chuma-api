Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Chuma is a community savings app for village banking and rotating savings groups (ROSCAs) in Zambia. This policy explains what personal and financial information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Chuma mobile app and this website.

Who we are. Chuma is operated by BRISTA-LITE ENTERPRISES LIMITED (“Chuma,” “we,” “us”), based in Zambia. For any privacy question or request, contact us at contact@bristalite.com.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run a savings group, verify who you are, move money safely, and support you.

CategoryWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Account & contact Your name, mobile phone number, profile photo (optional), the date you joined, and a hashed app PIN. To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
Identity verification (KYC) Your legal name, date of birth, government ID type and number (NRC or passport), and the result of the identity check. Verification is performed by our provider Didit; the ID image and selfie are handled by Didit and we receive the verification outcome and the extracted details above. To confirm your identity, meet financial and anti-fraud obligations, and protect group members' money. Required when you found a group.
Payment details Your preferred payout method (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Zamtel Kwacha, bank transfer, or cash), the account holder name, and the wallet or bank account number. To collect group fees and pay out savings, loans, and share-outs.
Group & financial activity The groups you belong to, contributions, savings, loans and repayments, penalties, transactions, and your calculated trust score. To operate your savings group and show accurate records to you and your group.
Device & technical A push-notification token (if you enable notifications) and basic technical logs needed to run the service. To send you alerts about approvals, contributions, and repayments, and to keep the app working and secure.
Stored only on your device. If you turn on biometric sign-in, the fingerprint or face check is handled entirely by your phone's operating system. Chuma never receives, sees, or stores your biometric data. Your app PIN is stored only as a secure one-way hash.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account, run your savings groups, and keep accurate financial records.
  • To verify your identity and prevent fraud, money laundering, and abuse.
  • To process contributions, fees, loans, repayments, and share-outs.
  • To send you notifications about your groups and account activity.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To comply with applicable laws and lawful requests from authorities.

Chuma is not a lender. Loans inside the app are made between members from their own group's pooled savings, on terms each group sets for itself. Chuma does not lend money and does not earn interest on member loans.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run Chuma:

We also share information with other members of your own savings groups where it is part of how the group works - for example, your name and your contribution and repayment activity are visible to your group so the group can be run transparently. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Chuma, our users, or the public.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your personal information while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal, accounting, and anti-fraud obligations. When you delete your account, we remove your personal and identity information as described in section 6. Some group financial records may be retained in anonymised form (showing only a name for the group's own history) so the group's books remain complete after you leave.

5. How we protect it

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Your PIN is stored only as a secure hash; sensitive values on your device use secure storage.
  • Access to systems is restricted, and identity documents are handled by our specialist KYC provider.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using reasonable safeguards.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: You can view and update your profile in the app, or contact us for a copy of your data.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and personal data from inside the app (Profile → Legal → Delete account), or request deletion at our account deletion page or by emailing contact@bristalite.com.
  • Withdraw participation: You can leave a group or stop using the app at any time.
Because Chuma holds money on members' behalf, we may need to keep your account open until you have settled outstanding obligations - such as repaying an active loan, or being refunded savings you still hold in a group - before we can delete it. We will always explain clearly what needs to be settled first.

7. Children

Chuma is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
