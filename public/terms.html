4. Loans between members

Some groups allow members to borrow from the group's own pooled savings. Where this happens:

  • The group sets the terms. The interest rate, borrowing limits, and repayment duration are set by the group, and any interest you repay returns to the group's fund and is shared among the members at share-out.
  • Loans require group approval. A loan is only issued once the group's required admins or members approve it.
  • Repayment is your obligation. You agree to repay on the agreed schedule. Late or missed repayments may incur penalties under your group's rules and may affect your trust score.
